Privacy Policy

Harbuz Multilingual Phrasebook · Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Artem Nagornyi ("I" or "the developer") handles information in connection with the Harbuz Multilingual Phrasebook Android app ("Harbuz" or "the app"; package name: com.arukai.uajpspeak). Harbuz is an ad-supported phrasebook and language-learning app provided free of charge.

Harbuz is designed to work offline after installation. An internet connection is used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request and display advertisements and may be used by your device's text-to-speech provider to download or provide online voices, depending on your device and voice settings.

1. Information stored on your device

Harbuz stores certain app information locally on your device so that its features can work. This includes your language preferences, favourite phrases, and flashcard learning progress.

This information remains on your device. Harbuz does not require an account, and the developer does not operate a server or cloud-sync service that receives or stores this information. It is not uploaded by Harbuz to the developer.

You can remove locally stored app information by clearing Harbuz's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app. The exact steps may vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

2. Advertising and information processed by Google

Harbuz uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. According to Google's documentation for the Android SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Depending on the SDK version, app configuration, device, and available identifiers, this information includes or may include:

  • IP address: may be used to estimate the device's general location.
  • App interactions: information about interactions with the app, such as app launches, taps, and ad views.
  • Diagnostics: information about app and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
  • Device and account identifiers: identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with accounts on the device.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Google also states that Android Advertising ID collection is optional and can be disabled by an app developer through the app's manifest configuration. The identifiers available to the SDK can also depend on Android settings and the device.

Google's handling of information, advertising preferences, and available privacy controls is described in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising and Privacy information.

3. Text-to-speech

Harbuz uses Android's system Text-to-Speech functionality to speak phrases. The app does not send phrase text to a server operated by the developer for speech synthesis. The text-to-speech engine and voice are supplied by Android, your device manufacturer, Google, or another provider selected or configured on your device. Depending on the selected engine and voice, speech may be generated entirely on the device, or the provider may process text online or download voice data. Any such processing is governed by the relevant provider's privacy policy and settings.

4. Information you send to the developer

Harbuz does not require you to submit personal information to use its phrasebook, favourites, or flashcard features. If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your message and address your request.

5. Third-party services

The principal third-party service integrated into Harbuz for advertising is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Android's text-to-speech engine may also be provided by Google, your device manufacturer, or another provider, depending on your device settings. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Google Play

6. Data retention and deletion

Language preferences, favourites, and flashcard progress stored by Harbuz remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Information collected or shared by Google Mobile Ads is handled under Google's retention and deletion practices. The developer does not receive or control all information processed by Google through its advertising services and cannot delete that information on Google's behalf. Please consult Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's data controls.

7. Security

Information stored locally by Harbuz is subject to the security of your device and its operating system. Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and the developer cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

Harbuz is a general-purpose phrasebook and is not specifically designed for children under 13. The app does not ask users to provide personal information to use its core phrasebook and learning features. The third-party advertising service described in this policy may process device and app information as explained above. Its handling of information and any applicable age-related advertising settings are governed by Google's policies and the app's advertising configuration.

If you believe a child has provided the developer with personal information by email, please contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Links to other websites

This app or this Privacy Policy may contain links to third-party websites. Those websites are not operated by the developer. Please review their privacy policies. The developer is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.
