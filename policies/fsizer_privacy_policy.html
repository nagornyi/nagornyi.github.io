Privacy Policy

F-Sizer Clothing Size Converter · Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Artem Nagornyi ("I" or "the developer") handles information in connection with the F-Sizer Android app ("F-Sizer" or "the app"; package name: com.anahorn.fukusizer). F-Sizer is an ad-supported clothing and shoe size converter provided free of charge. This policy replaces all earlier versions.

F-Sizer works offline: the size tables are bundled with the app, and conversions are calculated on your device. An internet connection is used by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request and display advertisements, and by Google Play to offer you an optional rating prompt.

1. Information stored on your device

F-Sizer stores your settings locally on your device so that the app can remember them. This includes whether you use women's or men's sizes, your home and shopping countries, your theme and language choices, whether sound feedback is enabled, and a few counters used to decide when to offer the rating prompt (the date of first launch, the number of conversions, and the date of the last prompt).

This information remains on your device. F-Sizer does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other personal information, and the developer does not operate a server or cloud-sync service that receives or stores this information. The sizes you convert are processed on your device and are not sent to the developer.

You can remove locally stored app information by clearing F-Sizer's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app. The exact steps may vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

2. Advertising and information processed by Google

F-Sizer uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. According to Google's documentation for the Android SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. Depending on the SDK version, app configuration, device, and available identifiers, this information includes or may include:

  • IP address: may be used to estimate the device's general location.
  • App interactions: information about interactions with the app, such as app launches, taps, and ad views.
  • Diagnostics: information about app and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate, and crashes.
  • Device and account identifiers: identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with accounts on the device.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). The identifiers available to the SDK can depend on Android settings and the device; you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings.

Google's handling of information, advertising preferences, and available privacy controls is described in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising and Privacy information.

3. Rating prompt (Google Play)

From time to time, F-Sizer may ask whether you would like to rate the app, using the Google Play In-App Review API. The prompt is displayed and handled by Google Play, which decides whether to show it. Any rating or review you submit is processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; the developer does not receive any personal information from the prompt itself. The app does not know whether or how you responded.

4. Information you send to the developer

F-Sizer does not require you to submit personal information to use its features. If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your message and address your request.

5. Third-party services

The third-party services integrated into F-Sizer are Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising and the Google Play In-App Review API for the rating prompt. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

Settings and counters stored by F-Sizer remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Information collected or shared by Google Mobile Ads is handled under Google's retention and deletion practices. The developer does not receive or control all information processed by Google through its advertising services and cannot delete that information on Google's behalf. Please consult Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's data controls.

7. Security

Information stored locally by F-Sizer is subject to the security of your device and its operating system. Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and the developer cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

F-Sizer is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed for children under 13. The app does not ask users to provide personal information to use its features. The third-party advertising service described in this policy may process device and app information as explained above. Its handling of information and any applicable age-related advertising settings are governed by Google's policies and the app's advertising configuration.

If you believe a child has provided the developer with personal information by email, please contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.
